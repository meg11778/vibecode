Explain this

The round is as many cards as you pick; players and speed come from the settings above. The other hands are dealt at random. A seat whose bid you set is given a hand on which the bid formula would make exactly that bid after the bids ahead of it. If you set the opening lead, the leader holds that card and the bots’ play engine would choose to lead it (or a card equivalent to it).

Set up an entire deal to study
Explain this

Give every player their cards, then bid and play it. Each bot still sees only its own hand. You can also set some or all of the bids and play some cards in advance, and start from that point. To play cards in advance, choose “Played cards” and click the cards in the order they were played; every hand must be complete, every bid set and trump settled first. Players and speed come from the settings above.
