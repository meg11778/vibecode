Oh Hell · play the bots

You against three, four or five bots, Vienna scoring, dealer hooked. The bots choose each bid by a short simulation and play their cards with the neural network. Every hand is saved so you can go back through their bids and their play.

New game

Set up a hand to study

Pick your cards; the round is as many cards as you pick. Players, bot bidding and speed come from the settings above. Everything below the cards is optional.

Bids, in bidding order (leave any at – to be bid at the table)

The other hands are dealt at random. A seat whose bid you set is given a hand on which the bid formula would make exactly that bid after the bids ahead of it. If you set the opening lead, the leader holds that card and the bots’ play engine would choose to lead it (or a card equivalent to it).

Set up an entire deal to study

Give every player their cards, then bid and play it. Each bot still sees only its own hand. You can also set some or all of the bids and play some cards in advance, and start from that point. Players, bot bidding and speed come from the settings above.
